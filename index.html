About Holt Robotic Systems

Holt Robotic Systems is an early-stage hardware company in central Illinois, developing its first product for agricultural drone spraying.

The company grew out of a working spray operation, and everything we build starts from what we've learned in the field.

We work with local fabricators, machine shops and students in central Illinois.
